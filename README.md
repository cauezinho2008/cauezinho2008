### 🚀 About Me

I'm a Brazilian 🇧🇷 tech enthusiast with a passion for understanding how things work — from the software running on a computer to the hardware powering it.

I'm fascinated by physics ⚛️, the universe 🌌, black holes 🕳️, and quantum mechanics. The idea of exploring the fundamental principles behind everything is simply amazing to me.

I've always been interested in tinkering with software 💾, hardware 🖥️, and electronics ⚡. More recently, I discovered the open-source community 🌐 and fell in love with Linux 🐧, digital freedom, and the philosophy of open-source software.

I started my Linux journey with Kubuntu and eventually moved to CachyOS, my current and favorite distribution. Along the way, I became increasingly comfortable with the terminal, started learning how Linux systems work under the hood, and developed a strong interest in system optimization, privacy 🔐, and performance 💯 — without sacrificing usability or aesthetics.

I'm currently learning **Bash scripting**, improving my command-line skills, and exploring the world of Linux system administration and automation. My goal is to understand the tools I use well enough to build my own solutions, rather than simply relying on ready-made ones.

I'm also the kind of person who enjoys disassembling, repairing, and upgrading computers and phones. Whether it's troubleshooting hardware, experimenting with software, or squeezing a little more performance out of a system, I genuinely enjoy the process.

Outside of technology, I'm a casual gamer 🎮, mainly playing Minecraft ⛏️ with performance and quality-of-life mods, alongside other games.

I'm also part of **Coelhos da Arte** 🎸, an original family band. We've been performing and creating music for eight years, and this year we started building our own home recording studio 🎙️. We're working on getting our music released across Spotify, YouTube Music, Deezer, SoundCloud, and other platforms.

Music is another big part of my life. I'm basically a rock-only listener 🤘, with bands like Metallica, AC/DC, Guns N' Roses, Nirvana, Lynyrd Skynyrd, and Dire Straits in my playlist, along with some French rock bands.

So, yeah... I'm basically a nerd 🤓

And, of course, my favorite color is **blue** 💙
